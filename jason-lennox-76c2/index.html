    Common questionsRebates, warranty, battery location and more
    What does "full-home backup" actually mean?

    During a blackout, your household circuits stay connected to the battery automatically. That doesn't mean every high-load appliance can run at the same time. Hot water, air conditioning, cooking appliances and EV charging still need to be managed during an outage.

    Are the rebates already included?

    Yes. Both prices already have the applicable rebates taken off. Final eligibility is confirmed as part of the approval process.

    Why is the price "preliminary"?

    We confirm your final price after checking your existing solar equipment, switchboard, roof, battery location, network approval and rebate eligibility. If anything changes, you'll know before we go ahead.

    What happens to my existing solar panels?

    Your existing solar is connected to the new GoodWe system, subject to inspection and testing on site.

    Who will actually install my system?

    Our own LCMB Group team. We don't use subcontractors. Your install is led by Harry, our SAA-accredited lead installer (S2068869), with Mack and Will on the crew.

    What warranty do I get?

    Your installation is covered by LCMB Group's 10-year workmanship warranty. That covers our installation work, alongside the manufacturer's warranties on the GoodWe equipment.

    Can I add more later?

    Yes. You can add solar or battery capacity later. If you think you'll grow your solar to around 10kW, Option 2 gives you more room to grow.

    Can I keep an eye on how it's performing?

    Yes. Both options include GoodWe SEMS+ monitoring. The app and web portal show live solar, battery and usage data, history and reports, and alert you to faults.
