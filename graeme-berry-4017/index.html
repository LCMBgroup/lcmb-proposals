    Common questionsRebates, inspection, backup and more
    Are the rebates already included?

    Yes. Both prices are approximate, include GST and have the applicable rebates included, subject to site inspection. Network approval and rebate eligibility are confirmed before anything proceeds.

    Why is the price "preliminary"?

    Luke will check a recent electricity bill (and the billing period for the $600 figure), your roof space, orientation and shading, whether the property is single-phase or three-phase, the switchboard condition, inverter and battery locations and cable routes before preparing the formal proposal.

    Which option should I pick?

    Luke recommends the larger system to start with, based on the bill discussed. The smaller system is the more economical option and may suit, depending on your electricity usage, roof space and whether you need blackout backup.

    Will the battery keep the power on in a blackout?

    Your blackout-backup requirements are one of the things Luke will go through with you at the site inspection, along with the final design.

    Who will actually install my system?

    Our own LCMB Group team. We don't use subcontractors. Your install is led by Harry, our SAA-accredited lead installer (S2068869), with Mack and Will on the crew.
