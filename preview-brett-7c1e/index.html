    Questions

    Good to know

    What does "full-home backup" actually mean?

    During a blackout, your household circuits can stay connected to the battery automatically, subject to your switchboard. That doesn't mean every high-load appliance can run at the same time, so air conditioning, ovens, hot water and pool equipment may still need to be managed.

    Are the rebates already included?

    Yes. Both prices already have the applicable rebates taken off. Final eligibility is confirmed as part of the approval process.

    Why is the price "preliminary"?

    Pricing is subject to a site inspection, the switchboard, network approval and rebate eligibility. If anything changes, you'll know before we go ahead.

    What happens to my existing solar panels?

    Both Fronius GEN24 inverters are removed and your existing panels are reconfigured onto the new GoodWe hybrid inverter, subject to inspection and testing on site.

    Who will actually install my system?

    Our own LCMB Group team. We don't use subcontractors. Your install is led by Harry, our SAA-accredited lead installer (S2068869), with Mack and Will on the crew.

    What warranty do I get?

    The GoodWe inverter and batteries include a 10-year manufacturer's warranty, and LCMB Group provides a 10-year workmanship warranty on the installation.

    Where will the battery go?

    Thanks for the photos of the three possible spots. Luke will compare them and lock in the final location with you when he comes out to check everything on site.

    Can I add more later?

    The GoodWe GW9.0 modules are stackable, so ask Luke at the site inspection about what could be added later for your setup.

    Can I keep an eye on how it's performing?

    Yes. Both options include GoodWe SEMS+ monitoring. The app and web portal show live solar, battery and usage data, history and reports, and alert you to faults.
