    Full scope of worksEverything in the price, stage by stage
    1. Site assessment and planning
    • Inspect the existing mains, switchboard and proposed cable route.
    • Confirm the new pole position next to the existing green supply pillar, allowing for access, underground services and the overhead route.
    • Check the attachment point on the front fascia and the structure behind it.
    • Review the kiln's electrical requirements alongside your existing loads.
    • Confirm the install sequence and the timing of the power interruption before work starts.
    2. Property pole and concrete foundation
    • Supply a new property pole to suit the overhead cable arrangement.
    • Set out the pole position after the site inspection and your underground services scan.
    • Excavate the pole hole, then position, align and concrete the pole in.
    • Install the pole-mounted fittings for the mains cable.
    3. Overhead cabling and house attachment
    • New point of attachment on the front fascia, fixed to suitable structure.
    • Riser bracket, mains connection boxes, mains clamps and attachment hardware.
    • New overhead aerial span from the pole to the house, supported to meet clearance requirements.
    4. Mains cable upgrade
    • Replace the existing 10mm² mains with new 16mm² single-phase mains from the new pole to the switchboard.
    • House-entry cabling from the attachment point to the switchboard along the agreed route.
    • Cable supports, entry fittings and terminations, and connection of the new mains.
    5. Switchboard component upgrade
    • One new 63A main switch for isolation of the mains supply.
    • One new busbar to suit the new switchgear.
    • Seven new RCBOs, giving each of your seven existing circuits its own breaker and safety switch.
    • Reconnection of the affected circuits, wiring adjustments and terminations.
    • Updated labelling of the circuits and equipment.
    • This is for replacing the listed components inside your existing switchboard enclosure.
    6. Testing, commissioning and handover
    • Electrical verification testing of the new mains and the switchboard work.
    • Checks of connections, circuit identification and the new protective devices.
    • Commissioning once the new supply is connected.
    • Certificate of Testing and Compliance for the work completed.
    • A walk-through of your new switchboard at handover.
    What you'll need to arrangeTwo things before the pole goes in
    • Tree trimming so there's access and clearance for the pole and the overhead cable route.
    • An underground services scan of the dig area, with the results sent to us before excavation.

    Both need to be done before the property pole can be installed.

    What's not includedSo there are no surprises
    • Tree trimming and underground services locating or scanning
    • The kiln circuit and the EV charger circuit (quoted separately)
    • Conversion or upgrade to three-phase supply
    • Energex or other network charges, meter changes, and work by Energex, your retailer or their contractors
    • Changes or upgrades to your existing solar or battery system
    • Repairs or reinstatement of walls, ceilings, driveways, retaining walls, gardens or landscaping, including painting and patching
    • Identifying, removing or disposing of hazardous materials, including asbestos
    • Fixing existing electrical defects, or any work outside the scope above

    If we find anything extra at the inspection or on the day, we'll talk it through and quote it for your approval before going ahead.

    How does payment work?

    No deposit is needed until the site inspection is done and you accept the final quote. After that it's a 50% deposit of $8,923.70, with the balance of $8,923.70 on completion. Bank transfer is preferred for payments over $1,000.

    When would the work happen?

    The works are planned for 2027. We'll lock in a date once the site inspection is done, your tree trimming and services scan are complete, and any network arrangements are confirmed.

    Will the power be off?

    Yes, there'll be a temporary interruption to your supply while the new mains are connected. We'll talk through the timing with you before we attend.

    Does this include the kiln and EV charger circuits?

    No. This quote gets your supply and switchboard ready for the extra load. The kiln circuit and the EV charger circuit will be quoted separately.

    Why is it subject to a site inspection?

    The final pole position, cable route and attachment point all depend on what we find on site and on the kiln's load. The final scope and price are confirmed before the works are booked, so you'll know before we go ahead.

    Does Energex need to be involved?

    Energex or retailer work, network charges and any meter change aren't included in this price. We'll let you know at the inspection whether any of these apply.

    What warranty do I get?

    Manufacturer warranties on the equipment, plus LCMB Group's 10-year workmanship warranty on the installation, and a Certificate of Testing and Compliance for the work.
